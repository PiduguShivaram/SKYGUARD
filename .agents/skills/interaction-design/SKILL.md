# Interaction Design Standards

## 1. Sensory Interaction Laws
1. **Continuous Feedback**:
   - An interactive element must respond to cursor or touch proximity before the click/tap happens (subtle illumination, magnetic drift).
2. **Direct Manipulation**:
   - The 24-hour weather scrubber must provide immediate 1:1 tactile tracking under the cursor, recalculating the sky state dynamically.
3. **No Dead Ends**:
   - Every alert card, location pill, and timeline hour must yield meaningful inspection data upon interaction.
