# Creative Art Direction Standards

## 1. Core Vision: The Living Sky
SkyGuard is an environmental intelligence medium, not an administrative tool. Its visual language reflects the atmosphere: fluid, expansive, luminous, and constantly changing.

## 2. Aesthetic Pillars
1. **Atmospheric Luma over Artificial Color**:
   - Palette derived from physical daylight transitions (golden dawn, crisp noon azures, twilight amethyst, midnight obsidian, storm-charge slate).
   - Reject arbitrary neon cyan/purple cyber gradients.
2. **Textural Depth & Tangibility**:
   - Utilize subtle micro-grain, atmospheric noise diffusion, and soft light scatter to ground visuals so they do not look like flat digital vector cutouts.
3. **Harmonic Scale Contrast**:
   - Contrast giant expressive typography (96px-120px) against hyper-refined, discrete metadata (10px-12px uppercase tracking).
4. **Cinematic Negative Space**:
   - Allow the atmosphere to breathe. Empty space is not "unused space to fill with cards" — it is the sky itself.

## 3. Anti-Patterns & Prohibitions
- **Generic SaaS Bento Grids**: Stacking equal-sized cards with tiny icons across the entire viewport.
- **Floating Decorative Geometries**: Hexagons, crosshairs, and fake radar discs that convey no genuine meteorological information.
- **Card Clutter**: Placing borders and backgrounds around every single sentence or metric.
