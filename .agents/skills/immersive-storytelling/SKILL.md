# Immersive Storytelling Standards

## 1. Narrative Arc Structure
A living digital experience unfolds as a curated journey rather than an overwhelming immediate dashboard dump:

- **Chapter 1: Your Sky (The Atmosphere)**:
  - Immediate sensory connection: The actual sky above the user's location rendered as a living, breathing light canvas.
  - Core truth: Location, temperature, and immediate severe advisories communicated in 2 seconds.
- **Chapter 2: Explore Your Day (The Timeline)**:
  - An interactive time-travel experience: Scrubbing the day dynamically shifts the solar angle, ambient illumination, and rain probabilities.
- **Chapter 3: A Changing World (The Earth's Pulse)**:
  - Discovery of planetary natural phenomena: Verified official alerts and disaster telemetry presented as a living pulse stream.
- **Chapter 4: Stay Prepared (Action & Assurance)**:
  - High-utility resolution: Clear recommendations, agricultural safety, and verified Web Push emergency notification controls.

## 2. Dwell-Time Mechanics
1. **Interactive Curiosity Loops**: Users are rewarded with visual feedback whenever they touch or scrub an element.
2. **Progressive Revelation**: Reveal deep meteorological data on demand rather than upfront clutter.
3. **Atmospheric Pacing**: Transitions feel like shifts in the weather — natural, fluid, and continuous.
