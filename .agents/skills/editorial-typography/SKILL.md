# Editorial Typography Standards

## 1. Typographic Contrast Architecture
1. **Display & Expressive Scale**:
   - Hero temperatures and atmospheric statements: 80px to 128px tabular numerals and elegant serif/sans letterforms.
   - Generous line height and optical tracking ensuring majestic presence.
2. **Editorial Narrative Copy**:
   - Prose reading like an environmental journal entry rather than a telemetry list.
   - Size: 18px-24px, relaxed leading (1.5-1.6), medium contrast foreground against midnight canvas.
3. **Scientific & Spatial Metadata**:
   - Coordinates, UTC timestamps, NWP models, and ISO codes: 11px-12px uppercase monospace (`font-mono tracking-widest`).

## 2. Typography Anti-Patterns
- Using all-caps everywhere.
- Generic un-tracked sans-serif labels above every number.
- Centered long body paragraphs that impair scanning.
- Mismatched font weights with harsh jarring transitions.
